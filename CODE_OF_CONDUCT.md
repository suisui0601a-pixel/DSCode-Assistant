# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our commitment

DSCode Assistant contributors and maintainers are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or location.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and technical honesty.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, documentation, and reproducible behavior.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and the ownership of source code and data.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes transparently.
- Keep Issues and Pull Requests relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sharing API keys, credentials, private source code, or personal data
- Deliberately misleading technical claims or fabricated test results
- Spam, sustained disruption, or abusive contact

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, and project-related communication channels.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `dscode.assistant@gmail.com`. Do not include credentials, private code, or unnecessary personal information. Maintainers will review reports in good faith and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify expectations, request corrections, remove inappropriate content, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude repeated or severe violations. Enforcement decisions should be documented internally and applied consistently.
